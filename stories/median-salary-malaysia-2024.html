How much do Malaysians earn?

Median & mean monthly salary in Malaysia, 2024

In 2024, the median monthly salary for Malaysian citizens was RM2,793 and the mean was RM3,652, up 7.3% and 6.1% from 2023.

What it means

  • The median (RM2,793) sits well below the mean (RM3,652), a sign that higher earners pull the average up, so "average salary" overstates what a typical Malaysian makes.
  • Salaries dipped in 2020 during the pandemic (median fell from RM2,442 to RM2,076) and only passed the pre-COVID level again around 2022–2023.
  • A tertiary education roughly doubles the median salary versus secondary (RM4,131 vs RM2,046).
  • A gender gap persists: women's median salary is about 7% below men's.

Frequently asked

What is the median salary in Malaysia in 2024?

The median monthly salary for Malaysian citizens was RM2,793 in 2024, according to the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM). The mean (average) was RM3,652.

What is the average salary in Malaysia?

The mean monthly salary was RM3,652 in 2024 (DOSM). Note the median, RM2,793, better reflects a typical earner, because a minority of high earners raises the average.

How much did salaries grow in 2024?

Median monthly salary rose 7.3% and mean rose 6.1% versus 2023, per DOSM's Salaries & Wages Survey Report 2024.
