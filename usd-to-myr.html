Common questions

What is 1 USD to MYR today?

The live mid-market rate is shown above (1 US Dollar in Malaysian Ringgit), updated each business day from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter.

Is this the bank exchange rate?

This is the mid-market reference rate. Banks and money changers add a spread, so the rate you actually get will be slightly different.

Why does the ringgit go up and down?

The ringgit moves with global factors, US interest rates, oil prices, trade flows and risk sentiment. The 30-day trend above shows the recent direction.

Where does this data come from?

Reference rates from the European Central Bank, served via the free Frankfurter API.
