Is Malaysia actually using AI?

21.8% of working-age Malaysians use AI tools, 51st in the world, a third of Singapore's rate, and climbing every quarter.

It depends how you measure it. Microsoft's AI Diffusion data (Q1 2026) estimates 21.8% of working-age Malaysians use AI tools, 51st of 147 economies, behind Singapore (63.4%) and Vietnam (26.5%) but ahead of the Philippines, Indonesia and Thailand. Local surveys read higher: Vodus found 67% of Malaysians say they used AI in the past 3 months, led by 25–34-year-olds. Both agree on the direction: adoption is rising every quarter.

What it means

  • By the strictest comparable measure, 21.8% of working-age Malaysians use AI tools, ranking #51 of 147 economies (Microsoft AI Diffusion, Q1 2026).
  • Singapore (63.4%) is in another league, nearly 3× Malaysia's rate and #2 in the world. Vietnam (26.5%) has pulled ahead with the region's fastest growth.
  • Malaysia still beats the Philippines (20.1%), Indonesia (14.1%) and Thailand (12.4%), solidly mid-table in ASEAN, mid-table globally.
  • Ask Malaysians directly and the number jumps: 67% say they used AI in the past 3 months (Vodus). Self-reported use of any AI reads higher than measured active use of AI tools, both are true, they measure different things.
  • The divide is age, not access: ~85% of 25–34-year-olds have used AI recently, versus only about a third of Malaysians 55 and older.
  • The trend is the story: +3.5 percentage points in nine months, rising every quarter.

Frequently asked

How many Malaysians use AI?

About 21.8% of working-age Malaysians actively use AI tools as of Q1 2026, per Microsoft's AI Diffusion data, while 67% of Malaysians say they used AI at least once in the past 3 months, per the Vodus panel survey. The first measures regular tool use comparably across countries; the second is self-reported recent use.

How does Malaysia compare to Singapore on AI adoption?

Singapore's AI diffusion is 63.4% of working-age adults (2nd in the world); Malaysia's is 21.8% (51st of 147). Singapore's rate is nearly three times Malaysia's.

Is Malaysia ahead of its neighbours on AI?

Mid-table: behind Singapore (63.4%) and Vietnam (26.5%), ahead of the Philippines (20.1%), Indonesia (14.1%) and Thailand (12.4%), as of Q1 2026.

Is AI adoption in Malaysia growing?

Yes, from 18.3% (H1 2025) to 19.7% (H2 2025) to 21.8% (Q1 2026) of the working-age population, a gain of 3.5 percentage points in about nine months.

Who uses AI most in Malaysia?

Young working adults: about 85% of Malaysians aged 25–34 used AI in the past 3 months. Among those 55 and above, roughly two-thirds have not used it at all (Vodus, early 2026).

Where does this data come from?

Microsoft AI for Good Lab's AI Diffusion Report, published as an open, MIT-licensed CSV on GitHub (147 economies, updated roughly quarterly), plus the Vodus AI Usage in Malaysia 2026 panel survey. Every figure links to its source.
