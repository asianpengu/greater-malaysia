Common questions

What is the petrol price in Malaysia this week?

The current RON95, RON97 and diesel prices are in the live panel above, updated every week. RON95 is held at its subsidised ceiling; RON97 and diesel float weekly.

Is RON95 still RM2.05?

RON95 is sold at a government-subsidised ceiling (around RM2.05/litre). The live figure above reflects the latest published price.

When do Malaysian fuel prices change?

Retail fuel prices are reviewed and announced weekly by the Ministry of Domestic Trade (KPDN) under the automatic pricing mechanism.

Where does this data come from?

Directly from data.gov.my (the official Malaysian open-data portal), sourced from KPDN.
