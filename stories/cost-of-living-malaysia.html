What's getting more expensive in Malaysia?

Inflation by category, where prices are rising fastest, 2026

Malaysia's headline inflation was +2.0% year-on-year as of May 2026, moderate overall, but the squeeze is uneven. Insurance & finance (+4.9%), personal care (+4.8%) and transport (+3.8%) rose fastest, while clothing actually got slightly cheaper.

What it means

  • Overall prices rose +2.0% in the year to May 2026, moderate, and below Malaysia's long-run average.
  • The real squeeze is in services: insurance & finance (+4.9%), personal care (+4.8%) and transport (+3.8%) led the gains.
  • Everyday food rose a milder +1.4%, and clothing was the only category to get *cheaper* (−0.1%).
  • "Headline inflation" is an average, your personal rate depends on what you spend on. A heavy driver feels transport; a renter feels housing.

Frequently asked

What is Malaysia's inflation rate?

Malaysia's headline inflation (CPI) was +2.0% year-on-year as of May 2026, according to the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM).

What is getting more expensive in Malaysia?

In the year to May 2026, insurance & finance (+4.9%), personal care (+4.8%) and transport (+3.8%) rose fastest. Food rose a milder +1.4%, and clothing got slightly cheaper.

What is the CPI?

The Consumer Price Index (CPI) measures the average change over time in the prices Malaysian households pay for a fixed basket of goods and services. DOSM publishes it monthly.
