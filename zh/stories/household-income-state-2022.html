Where you live shapes what you earn

Household income, poverty & the B40 / M40 / T20 split, 2022

Malaysia's median household income was RM6,338 a month in 2022, but it ranges from RM10,234 in Kuala Lumpur to RM3,614 in Kelantan. Nationally, the B40/M40/T20 lines fall at RM4,850 and RM10,960.

What it means

  • A Kuala Lumpur household earns about 2.8× more than a Kelantan one (RM10,234 vs RM3,614 median).
  • Whole states sit largely in the B40: Kelantan, Kedah, Perak and Sabah all have median household incomes below the national B40 ceiling of RM4,850.
  • Sabah has the highest poverty rate (19.7%) despite its natural resources; Putrajaya the lowest (0.1%).
  • The B40/M40/T20 labels are national cut-offs, what counts as 'middle income' in KL can be well-off in Kelantan.

Frequently asked

What is the median household income in Malaysia?

Malaysia's median household income was RM6,338 per month in 2022, according to the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM).

What is the B40, M40 and T20 income range in Malaysia?

By household monthly income (DOSM HIES 2022): B40 is RM4,850 and below, M40 is RM4,851–RM10,960, and T20 is RM10,960 and above.

Which Malaysian state has the highest household income?

Kuala Lumpur has the highest median household income (RM10,234/month in 2022), followed by Putrajaya and Selangor. Kelantan is the lowest at RM3,614.
