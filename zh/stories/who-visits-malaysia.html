谁来马来西亚旅游？

一半的入境者来自隔壁一个国家：新加坡。

大多来自隔壁。在一个普通的月份里，马来西亚迎来约300万名外国访客，其中约一半是越过长堤的新加坡人。印尼和中国紧随其后，但没有一个国家能接近新加坡。马来西亚最繁忙的边境是一座陆地桥梁，而非机场。

What it means

  • Half of Malaysia's visitors are Singaporeans: about 1.56 million in October 2024 alone, crossing the causeway to shop, eat and visit family.
  • The rest of the top ranks are regional neighbours: Indonesia (10.9%), Thailand (6.0%) and Brunei (3.2%). Proximity beats everything.
  • China is the big long-haul source at about 10%, far ahead of any Western country.
  • The top four are all Asian and make up 77% of arrivals. Western visitors (UK, Australia, US) are a small slice each.
  • It reframes 'tourism': Malaysia's visitor economy is overwhelmingly a short-haul, neighbourhood one, not a long-haul destination story.

Frequently asked

How many tourists visit Malaysia?

About 3.1 million foreign arrivals in October 2024, the latest published month, according to Immigration Department data on data.gov.my. Annual arrivals run into the tens of millions.

Which country do most Malaysia visitors come from?

Singapore, by a huge margin: around 50% of all foreign arrivals (about 1.56 million in October 2024), mostly crossing the land border. Indonesia (~11%) and China (~10%) are next.

How many Chinese tourists visit Malaysia?

China made up about 10% of foreign arrivals in October 2024, the largest long-haul source and third overall behind Singapore and Indonesia.

Where does this tourism data come from?

From the Immigration Department of Malaysia (arrivals by nationality), published as official open data on data.gov.my. Figures are for October 2024, the latest published month.
