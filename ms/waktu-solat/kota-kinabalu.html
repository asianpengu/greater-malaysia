Common questions

What time is Subuh in Kota Kinabalu today?

Subuh (Fajr) is shown in the live panel above and updates every day. The times come straight from JAKIM e-Solat, Malaysia's official Islamic authority.

What time is Maghrib in Kota Kinabalu today?

Maghrib (the time to break fast / berbuka) is in the live table above, updated daily from JAKIM e-Solat.

Which JAKIM zone is Kota Kinabalu?

Kota Kinabalu is covered by JAKIM zone SBH07, which includes Kota Kinabalu, Penampang, Putatan & Tuaran (zone SBH07).

Are these prayer times official?

Yes. Every time on this page comes directly from JAKIM e-Solat (Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia), the official source for prayer times in Malaysia.
