Common questions

How many public holidays does Malaysia have in 2026?

Around 15–18 national public holidays, plus extra holidays that vary by state. Employers must grant a minimum of 11 paid public holidays a year.

When is Hari Raya Aidilfitri 2026?

Expected on 21–22 March 2026, with related holidays on 20 March (additional) and 23 March (replacement). Islamic dates are subject to JAKIM confirmation.

When is Merdeka Day and Malaysia Day 2026?

Merdeka Day (National Day) is Monday, 31 August 2026. Malaysia Day is Wednesday, 16 September 2026.

Are these dates official?

This is the national list compiled from gazetted holidays. Islamic holiday dates are estimates pending moon-sighting, and states add their own holidays, always verify with the official MyGov calendar.
