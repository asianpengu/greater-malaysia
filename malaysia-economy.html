Malaysia's economy, in numbers

Malaysia's economy is worth about RM1.74 trillion a year and grew 5.4% in early 2026, driven by services and a world-class electronics manufacturing base. The federal government collects around RM315 billion a year and spends roughly RM407 billion, running a deficit it fills by borrowing. Frequently asked

How big is Malaysia's economy?

About RM1.74 trillion a year in real (inflation-adjusted) terms in 2025, and roughly RM1.9 trillion in nominal terms, per DOSM. It grew 5.4% year-on-year in the first quarter of 2026.

What is the biggest sector of Malaysia's economy?

Services, at nearly 60% of GDP, followed by manufacturing at about 23%. Agriculture, mining and construction make up the rest.

How much does the Malaysian government collect and spend?

It collected about RM315 billion in revenue in 2023 and spends roughly RM407 billion a year, so it runs a deficit funded by borrowing.

Is Malaysia a high-income country?

Malaysia is an upper-middle-income economy that has been approaching the World Bank's high-income threshold, dominated by services and a strong electronics manufacturing and export base.
