voter guide

Are you registered to vote?

Checking takes 30 seconds. Since automatic voter registration began in December 2021, most Malaysians aged 18+ are already registered, but you should confirm your details and find your polling station.

Updated 1 July 2026 · official source: MySPR Semak (SPR)

Check your status on MySPR Semak

The official Election Commission portal. Enter your IC number to see if you're registered, your constituency, and your polling station. Free and instant.

Check on MySPR Semak ↗

Step by step

1. Open the portal. Go to mysprsemak.spr.gov.my, or download the MySPR Semak app (App Store / Google Play).

2. Enter your IC number. Key in your MyKad number and the CAPTCHA code, then press Semak.

3. Read your details. You'll see your registration status, state and parliamentary seat, your polling station (pusat mengundi), your voting stream (saluran) and locality.

Do I still need to register?

Generally, no. Under automatic voter registration (AVR), in force since December 2021, Malaysian citizens turning 18 are added to the electoral roll automatically, the same reform (Undi18) that lowered the voting age from 21 to 18. It's still worth checking that your name and address details are correct, especially if you've moved.

Other ways to check

If you can't use the website, you can also download the MySPR Semak app, or call the Election Commission (SPR) hotline. Always use the official spr.gov.my domain, avoid third-party sites asking for your details.

Common questions

How do I check if I'm registered to vote in Malaysia?

Use the official MySPR Semak portal (mysprsemak.spr.gov.my). Enter your IC number and CAPTCHA, then press Semak. It shows your registration status, constituency and polling station.

Do I need to register to vote?

No, automatic voter registration since December 2021 registers citizens aged 18+ automatically. Still, check your details are correct.

How do I find my polling station?

Your polling station (pusat mengundi) and saluran are shown on MySPR Semak when you check with your IC number.

What is Undi18?

The reform that lowered the voting age from 21 to 18, implemented in December 2021 with automatic registration, adding millions of young voters.
